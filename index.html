Packaging machinery exhibition coverage and calendar

A machine running on a show floor settles questions a brochure cannot: how a bottle handles at speed, how quickly an operator changes format. Exhibitions are also where we sit down with distributor partners who are otherwise a time zone away. Three shows appear in our news so far.

Korea Pack and ICPI WEEK 2026, Goyang

Korea Pack 2026 was the 25th edition of South Korea's international packaging machinery exhibition. It ran from 31 March to 3 April 2026 at KINTEX in Goyang, with ICPI WEEK, a cosmetic and pharmaceutical show, held alongside it.

Our purpose was to meet our South Korean distributor. We held discussions at the partner's booth, toured the halls together and talked through where the two companies can work more closely. We attended as visitors, and the stand belonged to our partner.

The trip fits how we work in the region. South Korea is one of the markets where we have a local partner, and ICPI WEEK covers cosmetics and pharmaceuticals, two of the four industries on our solutions pages. Korea Pack runs on a two-year cycle, so the next edition falls in the future and its dates await the organizer's announcement.

Read the newsLocal partner in South KoreaCosmetics and personal care packaging

SWOP Shanghai 2024

SWOP Shanghai ran from 18 to 20 November 2024 at the Shanghai New International Expo Centre (SNIEC). We exhibited a labeling machine. By our team's own count, thousands of visitors stopped at the stand, and the show brought new contacts and potential partners.

If you are searching for this show, note the new name. From 2026 it runs as interpack China, part of the interpack alliance, on 16 to 18 November at the same venue. SWOP Shanghai and interpack China are the same event under two names.

Read the newsLabeling machines

K-PRINT 2024, Goyang

K-PRINT 2024 was the 27th Korea International Printing Machinery and Equipment Show. It ran from 21 to 24 August 2024 at KINTEX in Goyang, the same venue as Korea Pack. The show covers print, label, package, digital print, textile and signage, so it sits close to our labeling machines. Our news entry is titled "K-PRINT 2024 Exhibition Jvan Pack".

Customers who inspected their machines in Wenzhou

Customers who inspect a machine before it ships tend to ask sharper questions and sign off with fewer doubts. Two visits from Brazil are in our news so far.

November 2025, custom machinery. Brazilian customers came to Wenzhou with one aim: to see their custom machines in person before shipment.

8 May 2026, a granule packing line. A Brazilian customer and team toured the factory and were briefed on the layout, the quality control system and the production capacity. In the main workshop our technicians then started the whole line and ran it from bag feeding through granule filling and bag sealing to the final weight check.

The visitors kept their attention on five points:

  • Filling accuracy and speed
  • Changeover between bag sizes
  • Food-grade stainless steel on the parts that touch the product
  • Ease of operation and cleaning
  • Safety features and energy use

The entry describes the visit as a step toward closer cooperation in the Brazilian market. Two shipping entries for Brazil, a full container load and the loading of filling lines, have their own reports.

Customers who want their own samples run on the line can arrange a test through our team.

Meet the team

There are three ways to meet us.

  • At an exhibition. Send the show name, the day you plan to attend and your product. We will confirm whether the team will be there and arrange a time.
  • At the factory. Visit Wenzhou, walk the workshop and watch your own machine under test.
  • On a video call. We walk you through the workshop or run a test live, so you can request adjustments while the machine is still on our floor.

Whichever route you choose, send your product, your container and the output you need. Photos and samples are both useful.
